How ordering works

Personal, from the first hello.

1

Send an email

Tell Ashley which piece you would like, or what you would like her to design. Photos and ideas are welcome.

2

Design it together

She replies personally with ideas, materials and a total. Go back and forth until it feels exactly right.

3

Pay with Venmo

When you are happy with the plan, send payment by Venmo. Ashley makes your piece and ships it from her home studio.
